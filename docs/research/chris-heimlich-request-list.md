## Prioritized request, subject to the author's permission

1. **Consolidated MATLAB project**, including project file (`.prj`), top-level extraction/orchestration script, individual parsers, and any tests/docs. Ask if he can share **his own code**, and under what license or private-use terms.
2. **Name and mapping information**, including asset filename maps and (if available) `Dynamic_Entity_Name_Map.xlsx`, `Static_Entity_Name_Map.xlsx`, `Collision_Entity_Name_Map.xlsx` mentioned in S005.
3. **Unpublished research and corrections** since the blog posts: complex sprite decoder corner cases, entity IDs, collision shapes, scrolling/parallax, animation data, tile priority and gameplay behavior.
4. **Reference build identification**: original PC disc/release region and revisions, file sizes and hashes of `PITFALL.EXE`, DLLs and `.PH` files, if he has this information.
5. **Windows compatibility experiments**: CD audio, WAIL32.DLL and other dependencies, display, menus, timing and DxWnd.
6. **Test vectors**, if legally shareable: synthetic input cases or hashes/expected parser output metadata rather than redistributing copyrighted game assets.

## Handling and provenance

- Do **not** ask for or publish copyrighted game dumps unless rights are independently verified.
- Agree on sharing and licensing terms before incorporating someone else's scripts or data into the restoration.
- Keep author-supplied research separate from retail game content and capture provenance, version and permission in the inventory.
- Any reply should be logged factually with date, channel, what was offered, and any restrictions before claiming that material is available.
- Separate citations to public blog posts from validation with our own lawful retail files.
