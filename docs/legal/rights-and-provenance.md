# Rights and provenance policy

_This is a working policy, not legal advice._

## Ground rules

1. Owning a disc or installed copy does not grant the right to redistribute its code, art, music, video, text, or trademarks.
2. Original game material is used privately, for reference and testing only, and lives in `private/` (git-ignored).
3. Nothing original is downloaded from the internet. Only user-supplied material is used.
4. Code and assets we create ourselves are kept clearly separate from original material.
5. Before any public release, fundraising, or promotional use of names/logos, identify current rights holders and obtain appropriate permission or legal guidance.

## What may be committed to this repository

- Our own source code, scripts, and tests.
- Our own documentation, including *descriptions* of original file formats and behavior.
- Hashes and metadata identifying original files (not the files).

## What must never be committed

- Disc images, installers, original executables or DLLs.
- Original or extracted graphics, sounds, music, video, levels, or text.
- Screenshots or recordings of the original game, unless the user approves a specific, limited use.
- Credentials of any kind.
